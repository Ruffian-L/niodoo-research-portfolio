# Authorship

**Jason Van Pham** (Ruffian-L) is the project lead, corresponding human author,
research director, and the person who carried the design across tools, failures,

Contact: jasonvanpham@niodoo.com · [github.com/Ruffian-L](https://github.com/Ruffian-L)

## Position

Jason independently directed and integrated a continuous line of physics-inspired
retrieval, inference steering, memory, and control experiments with extensive
assistance from AI coding and research systems. The archive documents the work's
evolution, including false starts, corrected baselines, negative results, and
changes in interpretation. It does not allege copying by others or priority over
an entire field.

Named AI systems (Grok, Claude, Gemini, Codex, and local Niodoo instances) are
acknowledgments and contribution records. They are not co-owners of the project
and they do not decide what may be claimed.

## What counts as a paper in this git

A file is in this git because it documents dated work Jason directed, or because
it is a copy of a paper that already lives next to the code and receipts in a
public research repo.

A file is **not** in this git because an agent wrote it to satisfy extra
acceptance criteria on a plan. That is the contamination this portfolio is
built to refuse. See `provenance/CONTAMINATION.md` and `CATALOG.md`.

## Voice

Document the work. Rigor is receipts and honest losses — not a stack of
disclaimers that erases the milestone, and not a press release with no receipt.
Default shape: what happened, why it mattered, what the receipt is, then any
open item.
