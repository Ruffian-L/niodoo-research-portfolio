## How to mutate this map

1. Changelog first: what changed, **why**, what we think will happen (`CHANGELOG.md`).
2. Subject log in `research_logs/YYYY-MM-DD_slug.md`.
3. One row here: hypothesis, date, status.
4. If it is a result to take forward or already paid for, add a card under `silos/live/` or `silos/trail/` — not both.
5. Do not rewrite other papers to agree with the new one.
